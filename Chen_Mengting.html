Dr.Chen Mengting

Research Interest: Phytochemical, gut microbiota, cancer and chronic metabolic diseases.

Work Experience: Chongqing University Cancer Hospital, Department of Clinical Nutrition, Attending Physician, Dec 2020-Present.

Education:
Ph.D in Nutrition and Food Hygiene, Army Medical University, China, 2013-2019
BMed in Preventive Medicine, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, China, 2008-2013

Dr. Chen has successfully led multiple research projects funded by national and provincial grants, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China. She has a prolific publication record of over ten research articles, which have been cited more than 1000 times. She has also presented her work at international academic conferences, earning prestigious awards such as the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) Poster Award and the Japan Society of Clinical Oncology (JSCO) Best Abstract Award.
