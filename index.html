Research with us in Singapore

Yes Lab is based in the School of Chemistry, Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology (CCEB) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore (NTU Singapore).

About NTU Singapore

NTU is a research-intensive public university with disciplines spanning science, engineering, business, humanities, arts, social sciences, education and medicine. Its interdisciplinary environment brings together researchers with different perspectives and expertise.

About CCEB

CCEB brings chemistry, chemical engineering and biotechnology together in one interdisciplinary school. It connects science and engineering through education and research, providing a setting for work across molecular science, biological systems and their applications.
